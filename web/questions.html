A few questions?

The About page is the idea. This page is the rest of what people usually ask me once they have stood with it for a minute.

What does it cost?

I do not charge a price for storing the art. Ethereum does. Writing the file into the public record takes network fees, and those fees move with how busy the network is and how large the photograph is. Today’s fees for an open-size photograph are being read from the network…

Ethereum is changing how it charges to write something lasting. After that change, storing a photograph will cost more, because the network will ask more to keep new data in the record. The test network already uses the new charge. Mainnet still uses the older, cheaper one through March 2027. I will not pick a day for the switch. Through March 2027, plan on the mainnet figure. After that, expect the higher bill. The studio shows the live figure again before you send, because it moves. A larger photograph costs more, because more has to be written. Looking at the photographs costs nothing.

Why is the open size so small?

Anyone may upload photos up to ~74 KB. That is a small JPEG on purpose: the photograph’s data fits in one transaction, at a cost that stays in reach. A larger file takes more writes, more fees, and more steps. The pictures on the About page are packed to that size so you can see the resultant quality with your eyes, not as a number (e.g. 74 KB). Larger files are welcome, but the permission to upload them needs to be requested.

How do I store a larger photograph?

Send me the public address of the account that will store it, in a direct message on X to @A_is_A. I whitelist and verify legitimacy by hand. This boutique curation raises the upload size for approved accounts. Do not send a password, a seed phrase, or a private key. Only the public address.

Do I need an account to look, or to store?

To look, no. To store, yes. That account pays the network fee. You can name a different account as the holder of the work, so the person who pays and the person who holds the photograph do not have to be the same. I do not need a seed phrase, and you should not send me one.

Can the work be sold on other marketplaces?

No. The art minted or backed up here cannot be transferred or sold on other digital art marketplaces. That is the point for archives, gallerists, and independent artists who need control of sale and usage. If a work is listed, it is listed here, when you choose.

What is parking?

If you ever need to take a work out of your archive or off your wall, you can park it in secure long-term storage at an address of your choice, which is retrievable at any time. I previously used this vaulting mechanic in my project Stillwater. The parked work can only come home to the account it left.

What if this website disappears?

This page is only a door. If the door is ever destroyed, a new one can be easily created and the art pulled from the permanent provenance imparted by its Ethereum blockchain record. The file lives in that record. It does not live on my server.

Who holds the photograph?

The account named as owner at registration holds it. The account that stores it pays the fee. They can be the same person. I am not the owner of your photograph because I run this page.
