# Example photographs

These packed JPEGs sit at or under the open-tier cap of 73,728 bytes (four 18,432-byte pieces). They are for the hosted About page. The full-resolution files used to pack them live in the local `testing images/` folder and are not committed.

Provenance here means a surviving original plate, negative, or NASA frame whose copies now circulate without that object. The packed files are not those originals.
